# Homepage override

The homepage uses a sport-performance editorial system rather than the generated luxury/liquid-glass direction in `MASTER.md`. This override follows the client brief and supplied product assets.

## Visual language

- Ink `#132126`: primary type and dark performance sections
- Graphite `#26343A`: secondary surfaces
- Slate `#65757B`: supporting text
- Mist `#E9F0F1`: product and information surfaces
- Paper `#F7FAF9`: page canvas
- Teal `#009F98`: primary action and brand accent
- Bright teal `#00C9B7`: dark-surface accent and focus state

## Type

- Display: narrow, heavy athletic grotesk treatment for campaign headlines
- Body: open humanist sans for long-form readability
- Utility: mono face for speed data, labels, and product specifications

## Layout and signature

The layout pairs oversized campaign typography with real YG product photography and restrained technical-grid details. Its signature moment is one pinned GSAP throwing lane where a bag tracks from control to finish as the user scrolls. All other motion remains short, transform-based, and subordinate to shopping.

## Rules

- Avoid glassmorphism, generic gradient cards, decorative pills, and stock athletic imagery that is not specifically cornhole-related.
- Use no more than one pinned section.
- Keep all primary targets at least 44px and preserve visible keyboard focus.
- Disable smooth scrolling and scripted motion for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Verify at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 widths with no horizontal overflow.
